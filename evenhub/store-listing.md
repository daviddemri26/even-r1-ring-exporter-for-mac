# R1 Ring Data→ChatGPT

## Tagline

Connect R1 Ring Data to ChatGPT

## Description

This G2 app links to the Mac setup page. On your Mac, open https://is.gd/r1mac and click “Copy setup prompt”. Paste the prompt into Codex and press Return. Codex installs the Mac exporter and connects your ring data to ChatGPT. You handle sign-ins and permissions.

## Privacy

The G2 app shows setup instructions and asks for no device permissions. It does not read ring measurements. You can open the Mac setup page from the phone. [G2 app privacy policy](https://github.com/daviddemri26/even-r1-ring-exporter-for-mac/blob/main/docs/privacy.md#g2-app-privacy-policy)
