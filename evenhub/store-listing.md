# R1 Ring Guide for G2

## Store copy

**Tagline**

Connect your R1 ring data to ChatGPT.

**Description**

Set it up once on your Mac. Then ask about your ring’s sensor readings right in the chat. Open the setup guide on your phone to get started.

**Release notes for 0.2.0**

A single screen introduces the connection. Open the Mac setup guide from your phone.

[Glasses screen captured in the Even Hub simulator](media/g2-connection-screen.png)

**Privacy**

The glasses app displays a message and a link. It requests no device permissions and reads no measurements. [Project privacy guide](https://daviddemri26.github.io/even-r1-ring-exporter-for-mac/guides/privacy/)
